DSCR vs. conventional loans for rental property: which fits?

Jayson Cain, Mortgage Loan Originator, NMLS #2270200 Updated October 4, 2026· 6 min read

Short answer

If your tax returns show strong income and you're buying in your own name, check conventional first. If write-offs shrink your taxable income, you want to buy in an LLC, or you're past a handful of financed properties, DSCR may fit better. The best answer depends on the property's rent, your tax returns and how many properties you plan to own.

Why DSCR is a business-purpose loan

A DSCR loan finances a rental you won't live in. Under the federal Truth in Lending Act, credit extended primarily for business purposes is exempt from many consumer lending rules. That's also why a DSCR lender can focus on the property instead of your personal income.

What to check before you choose

  • Rent-to-price ratio. In lower-cost markets the rent often covers the payment easily, which makes DSCR simple. In high-cost metros it may not.
  • Your tax returns. If they show strong income after write-offs, conventional is worth pricing out.
  • How many properties you'll own. Fannie Mae caps financed properties at 10. If you're scaling, plan for DSCR.
  • How long you'll hold it. DSCR prepayment penalties matter if you might sell or refinance in the first few years.
  • Local rental rules. Rent control and short-term rental limits vary by city and affect the rent a lender will count.

When conventional usually fits

  • Your tax returns show income that supports the payment
  • You're fine holding title in your own name
  • You own fewer than 10 financed properties
  • You might sell or refinance soon and don't want a prepayment penalty

When DSCR usually fits

  • Write-offs lower your taxable income
  • You want to close in an LLC
  • You're scaling past conventional limits
  • The rent covers the payment, or close to it, with the down payment you have

Quick answers

Can I buy a rental in an LLC with a conventional loan?

Generally no. Conventional loans sold to Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac are made to individuals. DSCR loans commonly allow an LLC.

Can I refinance from DSCR to conventional later?

Often yes, if you qualify and the property is in your name. Check the prepayment penalty first.

Can I get a DSCR loan for an out-of-state rental?

Often yes. Many DSCR lenders finance rentals in multiple states, and investors often buy where the rent covers the payment more easily. Availability depends on the lender and the state.
